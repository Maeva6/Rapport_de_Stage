\chapter{Architecture diagram}
\label{annexe:architecture}

Figure~\ref{fig:archi-annexe} shows the platform at the time of writing: the physical server runs two virtual machines, each of which opens its own outbound tunnel to Cloudflare. No inbound port is opened on the network, which is what makes publication possible behind the shared Starlink connection (CGNAT).

\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[

  arr/.style={-{Latex[length=2mm]}}
]
\node[box, minimum width=10.5cm] (users) at (0,0) {Users on the Internet (browser, mobile data)};

\draw[arr] (users) -- (cf);

\node[vm] (dvm) at (-2.9,-5.3) {\textbf{deploy-vm}\\[2pt]\footnotesize Supervision dashboard (Docker)\\ Jenkins\\ Traefik (kept for a public IP)\\ \texttt{cloudflared} container};
\node[vm] (gvm) at (2.9,-5.3) {\textbf{gitlab-vm}\\[2pt]\footnotesize GitLab CE (Docker)\\ Runners for the other interns\\ \texttt{cloudflared} container};

\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Architecture of the platform: physical server, virtual machines, containers and tunnels.}

\end{figure}

Published addresses: \texttt{dashboard.ucac-icam.info} (dashboard, tunnel \texttt{info-control-center}) and \texttt{gitlab.ucac-icam.info} (GitLab, tunnel \texttt{info-gitlab}). Git over SSH (port 2222) and the administration interfaces of Jenkins and Traefik stay on the local network.
