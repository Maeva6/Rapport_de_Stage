\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

As part of the fourth year of my computer science programme, I completed a DevOps-oriented internship at Ucac-Icam, under the supervision of Fr. Maximilien Bossou and Mr. Christian Aboudi. The assignment given to me was deliberately broad in scope but precise in intent: \emph{setting up, configuring and deploying a secure web server, and hosting multiple web applications on it}. In practice, this meant turning a physical server that was sitting idle into an operational, durable, and adequately secured hosting platform for internal projects.

Rather than a single fixed specification handed down at the start, the project grew iteratively: each milestone (authentication, HTTPS, firewalling, continuous deployment) surfaced the next one, and several early assumptions had to be revisited once they met the reality of the actual network the server operates on. This proved to be a formative constraint in itself, closer to how infrastructure work is actually conducted in production than a textbook exercise would have been.

This report is organised in four parts. Part 1 presents the context of the internship and the infrastructure available at the outset. Part 2 details the requirements and the technical choices made to meet them. Part 3, the core of the report, walks through the technical implementation in the order it was actually built: base infrastructure, security hardening, continuous integration and quality, multi-application hosting, and supervision. Part 4 draws a balance sheet of the work completed, the difficulties encountered, and the skills gained, before a conclusion closes the report.
